How do I start?

Import audio from Files, record with the microphone, or import a video to extract its audio. The included Bach sample lets you explore the tools before using your own files.

What can I edit?

Zoom and select a range, keep or delete that range, split or duplicate clips, and merge clips in order. Volume, peak normalization and fades help finish the sound. Use undo if you want to step back.

Which formats can I export?

Export as M4A or WAV, then save or share through the iOS share sheet. Supported imports depend on the media formats iOS can read. WaveWhale does not export MP3.

How does the free experience work?

You can fully edit one personal project at a time without a time limit, including one successful export. Cancelling or failing an export does not use the allowance. Before a successful export, deleting the project lets you start again. After that export, deleting or resetting projects does not restore the allowance. The built-in sample remains free.

Subscriptions, trial and lifetime access

Monthly, yearly and lifetime access unlock the same tools and remove project and export limits while access is valid. A 7-day subscription trial is offered only when Apple confirms eligibility and you confirm the subscription. It automatically renews at the price Apple displays unless cancelled. This is separate from the one-free-export experience, which needs no subscription and never renews. Lifetime access is a separate one-time purchase. Buying it does not cancel an existing subscription; manage that separately with Apple.

Restore, cancel or request a refund

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To avoid renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period or trial ends. Apple handles refund requests through Report a Problem. Access follows Apple’s verified purchase status.

Recording or playback trouble?

Check microphone permission in iOS Settings, available device storage and the current audio output. Stop other apps using the microphone and try again. Keep the app open while a recording or export is running. Save important files separately before troubleshooting.

Still need a hand?

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot without personal information can help. Please describe audio issues without uploading confidential recordings. We can respond in English or Chinese.

Contact support on GitHub ↗

GitHub Issues are public and require a GitHub account. Please do not attach private recordings, payment details or other sensitive information.
